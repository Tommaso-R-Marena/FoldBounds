# Robust conformational-event switching under model-energy uncertainty

**Status:** Mathematically exact conditional on the **specified finite lattice Hamiltonian and error assumptions**. It does **not** predict real protein-fold populations or establish a field-level breakthrough.

## Motivation and experimental target

The previous FoldBounds ensemble-crossover experiments certified whether a hydrophobic-contact event occupies a majority of equilibrium conformations across ranges of contact and bend weights. That is not enough for physical reliability: real proteins have unknown energy-model errors, possible missing conformations, solvent effects, and environmental parameter uncertainty. This research asks whether a majority conclusion **survives an explicitly bounded error in Boltzmann weights**.

The published GA95 and GB95 56-residue proteins (RCSB 2KDL and 2KDM; Alexander et al., PNAS 2009) are a future experimental target for this kind of method. Their observed NMR fold differences do not validate the present toy model. A 2025 Chemical Science study implicated aromatic interactions including F30, Y45, and F52 in their fold switching; an atomistic, experimentally calibrated model is needed before we can predict or certify GA/GB populations.

## Exact mathematical theorem (informal proof, tested Python)

Let C be a finite nonempty conformation space. Each state x has a nominal positive weight w(x), giving event mass E and complement mass N, and event population p=E/(E+N). The true weight is w'(x)=f(x)w(x), where each (possibly adversarially correlated) correction factor satisfies **0 < l ≤ f(x) ≤ u**. Then:

\[
  \boxed{\frac{lp}{lp+u(1-p)} \le P'(A) \le
  \frac{up}{up+l(1-p)}}.
\]

The bounds are *sharp*: assign all event states factor l and all non-event states factor u to attain the lower bound, and reverse these assignments to attain the upper bound. There is no independence hypothesis. A common multiplicative correction cancels identically, demonstrating the importance of **relative, not absolute**, energy uncertainty.

If the nominal histogram is generated by an HP contact/bend lattice energy:

\[
  w(x;q,b)=q^{K(x)}b^{R(x)},\quad q>0, b>0,
\]

and the exact coefficient witness for \(\partial_bP\ge0\) succeeds throughout a rectangle in q,b (alongside the general event-threshold monotonicity \(\partial_qP\ge0\)), the extremes over the entire continuum of \((q,b)\) and all allowed f(x) occur at the q,b rectangle corners, with event/complement factors chosen adversarially. This turns an infinite-dimensional adversarial perturbation problem into *two exactly checkable rational expressions*.

The event here is **at least a specified number of H-H lattice contacts**, not a distinction between two experimentally observed fold topologies. The term “switch” means a majority crossover of this precisely defined event.

## Certified ambiguity band for a majority crossover

For fixed q, let E(b), N(b) be the exact rational-weight polynomials. The nominal majority crossover is E=N. Given corrections l,u:

- **Guaranteed minority** at b where \(u E(b)<l N(b)\).
- **Guaranteed majority** at b where \(l E(b)>u N(b)\).
- Between the unique positive roots of \(uE-lN=0\) and \(lE-uN=0\), both majorities are possible under allowed corrections.

For each root, a *single positive root* is established by Descartes' sign rule (exact polynomial coefficients and one sign change); strict endpoint signs and rational bisection isolate it. Nonnegative coefficients of the q,b-shifted derivative numerator prove the required monotonicity on the relevant parameter domain. If a proof obligation fails, the checker rejects rather than guessing.

### Conditional bridge to verified finite-space thermodynamic mass bounds

If a *separate trusted checker*, e.g., a Lean-verified FoldBounds frontier checker, has established E∈[E_low,E_up], N∈[N_low,N_up], the reweighted event population obeys

\[
  \frac{lE_{low}}{lE_{low}+uN_{up}} \le P'(A) \le
  \frac{uE_{up}}{uE_{up}+lN_{low}}.
\]

If either relevant ratio's denominator is zero, the Python implementation returns conservative zero/one bounds; an entirely zero upper partition mass is rejected. This is a **sound mass-bound propagation rule conditional on input soundness**, not proof that an untrusted mass oracle or physical force field is valid.

The new implementation includes a *Lean theorem* for the strict-positive-lower-mass case only **if successfully compiled and audited in CI**; do not infer from Python tests that Lean certification has succeeded.

## Falsification and independent checks

- 22 parameterized Python tests, including a complete 30,073-walk independent exact reweighting of both event and complement classes.
- Randomized 2D/3D histograms under independently varied q,b and rational state-class correction factors, all enclosed.
- Exact rational majority root brackets with Descartes' sign check, monotonicity coefficient proof, and uniform-error cancellation.
- Forged coefficients, incorrect bounds, invalid dimensions, non-rational parameters, and unsupported monotonicity certificates rejected.
- Full experiment regenerated from source into `experiments/robust_switch_results.json`.
- **Negative result:** a 20%-factor systematic error allowance greatly widens crossover ambiguity. Nominally precise crossover numbers without credible residual-energy error bounds are not physical predictions.

## Limitations and next genuine test

No experimental energy bound l/u has been established. The 2D and 3D models do not include electrostatics, solvent, hydrogen-bond directionality, entropic solvent free-energy, post-translational modifications, or atomistic kinetics. Shape-independent statewise factors are a deliberately broad **worst-case model**; this may be pessimistic when errors are correlated and does not capture unknown conformations. The experimental GA95/GB95 NMR structures are published, but this computation did not download or analyze their atom coordinates because the current runtime could not access the source PDB data. No RMSD, native-contact or force-field result is asserted.

Next steps: (a) atomistic force-field or potential-of-mean-force uncertainty *calibrated* on genuinely held-out NMR ensemble benchmarks, (b) compact bounds on unsampled conformation masses, (c) Lean-proved propagation from `FrontierNext.check_sound` to these reweighting bounds, and (d) prospective GA/GB population discrimination against NMR and established ensemble methods.
